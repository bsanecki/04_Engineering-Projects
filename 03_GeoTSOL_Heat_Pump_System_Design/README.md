# GeoT*SOL Heat Pump System Design

## Project Overview

A comparative study of residential air-to-water heat pump systems developed using **GeoT*SOL 2025 R1**. The project includes four simulation scenarios covering two household sizes (2+2 and 2+4) in two Polish locations: Rzeszów and Olsztyn.

The simulations focus on space heating, domestic hot water preparation, photovoltaic energy generation, electricity consumption, system performance and environmental impact.

## Scope of Work

- Configuration of residential heat pump systems.
- Definition of building heating demand and domestic hot water requirements.
- Selection of heat pump and hot water storage components.
- Simulation of annual energy consumption and system performance.
- Analysis of photovoltaic energy generation and self-consumption.
- Comparison of results across locations and household sizes.
- Evaluation of primary energy savings and avoided CO₂ emissions.

## Privacy Notice

All personal information included in the PDF reports, including names, surnames, addresses and other identifying details, is fictional and was created solely for demonstration purposes. No real individuals or private addresses are represented.

## Technical Note

Although the original report titles refer to geothermal systems, the simulated configurations use ambient air as the heat source and air-to-water heat pumps.

All presented results are software simulation outputs based on the assumptions and input parameters documented in the individual PDF reports. They do not represent verified real-world operating data or a certified installation design.
