# PV*SOL Photovoltaic System Design

## Project Overview

A comparative study of residential photovoltaic (PV) systems designed and simulated using **PV*SOL 2025**. The project includes four scenarios covering two household sizes (2+2 and 2+4) in two Polish locations: Olsztyn and Rzeszów.

The study focuses on PV system configuration, component selection, annual energy production, self-consumption, grid exports, energy self-sufficiency and financial performance.

## Scope of Work

- Configuration of grid-connected residential photovoltaic systems.
- Selection of PV modules and inverters.
- Definition of module orientation, tilt angle and installation area.
- Configuration of electrical connections and inverter inputs.
- Analysis of local climate data and solar irradiation.
- Simulation of annual electricity production.
- Evaluation of self-consumption and energy exported to the grid.
- Analysis of energy self-sufficiency and avoided CO₂ emissions.
- Financial analysis, including investment costs, IRR and estimated payback period.
- Comparison of system performance across different locations and household scenarios.

## Privacy Notice

All personal information included in the PDF reports, including names, surnames, addresses, company details and other identifying information, is fictional and was created solely for demonstration purposes. No real individuals or private addresses are represented.

## Technical Notes

The reports were generated using PV*SOL 2025 Non-commercial editions. The simulations use climate data from Meteonorm 8.2.

The 2+4 reports contain a household load profile labelled as a one-person household. This input inconsistency should be considered when interpreting the energy consumption, self-consumption and self-sufficiency results.

All presented values are software simulation outputs based on the assumptions and input parameters documented in the individual PDF reports. Actual system performance may differ due to weather conditions, equipment efficiency and installation-specific factors.
